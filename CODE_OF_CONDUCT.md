# Code of conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to making participation in the `single-auth` community a
respectful and harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age,
body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics,
gender identity and expression, level of experience, education,
socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or
sexual identity and orientation.

We will act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming,
diverse, inclusive, and healthy community.

## Expected behavior

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive community include:

- showing empathy and respect for different viewpoints and experiences;
- giving and accepting constructive, specific technical feedback;
- focusing discussion on observable behavior, evidence, and project scope;
- accepting responsibility, apologizing to those affected by mistakes, and
  learning from the experience;
- prioritizing the health of the community and the security of its users.

## Unacceptable behavior

Examples of unacceptable behavior include:

- sexualized language or imagery, unwanted sexual attention, or advances;
- insulting or derogatory comments, personal or political attacks, and
  sustained disruption;
- public or private harassment;
- publishing another person's private information without explicit permission;
- exposing credentials, session material, personal data, or vulnerability
  details through public project channels;
- conduct that could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional
  setting.

Strong disagreement about architecture or compatibility is allowed. Abuse of
contributors is not.

## Enforcement

Repository maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing these
standards. They may edit or remove comments, commits, code, issues, and other
contributions that violate this code, and may temporarily or permanently ban a
participant for behavior they consider inappropriate, threatening, offensive,
or harmful.

Report conduct concerns privately through the maintainer contact route
described in [`SECURITY.md`](SECURITY.md). Do not disclose sensitive personal
information in a public issue. Maintainers will respect the privacy and
security of the reporter as far as reasonably possible.

## Scope

This code applies within project spaces and when an individual is officially
representing the project in public spaces.
